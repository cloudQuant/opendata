## why the batch is two rows and not three

Task #55 was armed as "government_us 3 rows (CommodityPsdData / CommodityPsdReport /
WeatherBulletinDownload)". Measurement cut it to two:

* The witness module's own docstring had excluded the two PSD rows: "they refuse with
  USDA_FAS_API_KEY_MISSING_OR_INVALID before any transport, so a witness for them would be a witness
  of credential plumbing". That exclusion is false on the current code. The credential is satisfiable
  through the runner's `credential_env` injection (the eia witness already does it), and both rows
  carry a post-response guard the fixture can reach -- `commodity_psd_data.py:186`
  (`USDA_BAD_COMMODITY_ROW`) and `commodity_psd_report.py:104` (`USDA_REPORT_NOT_PDF`). Reversed in
  the module docstring as a correction on the record.
* `WeatherBulletinDownload` stays out, but the *reason* the module carried for it was also false, and
  the first draft of this carrier repeated it. Measured: it declares no `row_model`
  (`weather_bulletin_download.py:91` prints `canonical_model` only) and its nine `raise` sites --
  :33, :36, :51, :54, :63, :70, :75, :77, :112 -- are all bare `ValueError` with an `NWS_*` message,
  every one a query/URL-shape guard that fires before transport. The shipped counterfact mechanism
  tampers the fixture *document* (`tamper.path` always starts at `responses`), so no tamper can reach
  a refusal the query itself must trip. The module docstring now says this; the previous wording
  ("its only rejections are the `GOV_US_*` transport codes") is superseded, not deleted.

Both PSD rows also needed a `row_model` declaration -- the same one-line form shipped on
`treasury_auctions.py:63` and `weather_bulletin.py:47`.
