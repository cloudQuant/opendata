## What was found

`provider_source_review_evidence.py` pins `EXPECTED_BASELINE_PROVIDERS = {ecb, fred, imf, oecd,
yfinance}`. The repo now registers **twelve** self-developed provider packages. For `bls`, `cboe`,
`federal_reserve`, `fmp`, `sec` — five of them — the OpenBB counterpart sits unused in the same pinned
checkout, so the screen compared our blocks against a baseline that cannot contain their counterpart
at all. A zero over an empty matched scope is a zero without a denominator.

Measured, both arms (same 521 local blocks, same pinned checkout, same `EXPECTED_OPENBB_COMMIT`):

| arm | baseline providers | baseline blocks | pairs | candidates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 (as shipped) | ecb, fred, imf, oecd, yfinance | 1,105 | 520 | 8 |
| 10 (widened) | + bls, cboe, federal_reserve, fmp, sec | 2,844 | 520 | 14 |

Widening does change the answer: 6 more candidate rows, and 2 of them sit **inside the five added
packages** (`fmp/models/equity_historical.py::EquityHistoricalQuery._validate_window` at 0.7330 and
`bls/models/_client.py::._year_windows` at 0.7185). Those two rows were invisible to the shipped
arm by construction, not by absence.

## The second scope hole, found while reading the arms

`local blocks per package` printed **9** packages, not 12: `cboe`, `federal_reserve`, `sec` contribute
zero blocks in *both* arms. `measure_tree` keeps only blocks over 40 nodes, and those three packages
are engine-declarative (9, 8 and 6 tracked `.py` files — specs and a registration module, no function
bodies above the floor). So for those three the screen's local side is empty regardless of the
baseline set. This is not a defect in the screen — it is a statement about what the screen cannot be
evidence for, and it belongs in AC-10|03's reading next to the baseline half.

## Control: the screen must be able to fire

The clone control injects one of our own blocks into the baseline as a fake upstream file
(`dataclasses.replace` on the biggest block, 976 nodes, `ths/transport/http_client.py::FuyaoHttpClient
._get_locked`, filed under `CONTROL/clone-control.py`) and demands it come back as the top pair:

- `control_score: 1.0`, `control_ranked_first: true`, `control_is_candidate: true`,
  `control_side_of_row: baseline` — the screen caught its own injected clone.
- `contrast: best real nearest in arm10 scores 0.7435`, so the control is not a coincidence of the
  ordering.

Without this arm the 14 candidates in arm10 and the 0 in the untouched packages would be
indistinguishable from a screen that cannot fire.

The first attempt at this control **crashed** (documented in the harness docstring, not hidden): the
control path was outside `openbb_platform/providers`, so `Block.is_baseline` — a property derived from
the path, not a field — filed the injected block as *local*, and the shipped `_pair_sort_key` raised
`KeyError: 'baseline_path'`. The fix is the path, not the sort key.

## Decision this face supports

Widen `EXPECTED_BASELINE_PROVIDERS` to the providers whose counterparts exist in the pinned checkout,
as part of task #47's bundle rebuild over the 12-package census — and carry the 9-of-12 local-block
census in the same reading, so "matched scope" names both denominators. Until that rebuild lands,
AC-10|03's `NO_COMPLEX_COPY_OBSERVED_IN_MATCHED_SCOPE` covers 5 baseline providers and 9 local
packages, and this file is the face that says so.

What this face does **not** claim: that the 14 candidate rows are benign. They are below the
「complex copy」 bar the screen states (`CANDIDATE_DICE 0.7`, `CANDIDATE_NODE_RATIO 0.55`,
`CANDIDATE_ORDERED_RATIO 0.62`), and rows that need a per-pair review finding are task #47's work,
not this run's.
